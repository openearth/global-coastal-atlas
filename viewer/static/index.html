Global Coastal Atlas viewer

Layers published in the GCA STAC catalog (collections 17-31), shown for an area of interest. WMS layers come from GeoServer; vector layers are read from GeoParquet for the bounding box. Soil Zarr depth cubes are drawn by the viewer: pick a depth interval, click the map for a depth profile, or draw a transect to get a section through the depth dimension.
